Rules

  • Colour only in a terminal. Off when piped, with NO_COLOR or TERM=dumb; FORCE_COLOR=1 turns it on.
  • Status never by colour alone: the mark says it too.
  • Every problem ends with the command that fixes it.
  • Your home folder shows as ~. Lines fit in 80 columns.
  • doctor and daemon status take --json, for scripts.
